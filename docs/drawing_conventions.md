# Drawing conventions

- **Units:** model-space plan dimensions are in inches; layouts use A3 landscape paper coordinates.
- **Overall master sheet:** A-001, 1:100 at A3.
- **Enlarged dimension sheets:** A-002–A-005, 1:75 at A3.
- **Combined dimension sheet:** A-007, 1:100 at A3.
- **External dimensions:** wall centerline to wall centerline.
- **Internal clear dimensions:** face of wall to face of wall.
- **Windows:** cyan/glazing indicators where used; wall end caps and wall terminations are black.
- **Walls:** use the existing multi-line wall convention; properly trim/join faces at corners and T-junctions.
- **Doors and bifolds:** retain established symbols and directions, including the outward-opening, right-hinged master bathroom door.
- **Labels:** centered within usable room area, avoiding dimension text and walls. MASTER / BEDROOM is presented as two centered lines.
- **Annotations:** preserve dedicated dimension layers, keep clear of room labels, door swings and windows.
- **Title block:** preserve the original multi-cell A3 architectural title block; modify text only. Use US English (e.g. centerline).

Important: The model-space annotations are composed of CAD lines and text, **not** associative dimensions. Paper-space sheet copies are non-associative. Editing model geometry does not automatically update their dimension labels or copied paper-space graphics.
